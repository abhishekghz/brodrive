      About the trip

      Amritsar is a must for every visitor to Punjab – the Golden Temple at dawn, Kesar Da Dhaba lunch and the evening Beating Retreat at Wagah Border.

      A same-day round trip is possible if you leave early, but we recommend two days to take it all in. Your driver knows the Wagah timings and the best spots to park near Harmandir Sahib.

      Chandigarh to Amritsar cab FAQs

      What is the cab fare from Chandigarh to Amritsar?

      A one-way sedan is about ₹3,060 and a same-day round trip about ₹5,820, including driver. Tolls and parking are extra.

      Can I visit Wagah Border on the same trip?

      Yes. Wagah is about 30 km from Amritsar city; the extra distance is added to your per-km fare.

      How long is the drive from Chandigarh to Amritsar?

      Around 4 to 4½ hours by road.
