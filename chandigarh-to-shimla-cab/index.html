About the trip

Shimla is Chandigarh's favourite weekend escape, and the four-lane highway now makes it a comfortable 3-hour climb. Hill-trained BroDrive drivers handle the bends while you enjoy the views from Parwanoo upwards.

Book a one-way drop for a hotel check-in, or a round trip with Kufri and Mashobra sightseeing. For a family of six, the Ertiga or Innova is the comfortable pick.

Chandigarh to Shimla cab FAQs

What is the cab fare from Chandigarh to Shimla?

A one-way sedan is about ₹1,860 and a same-day round trip about ₹3,300, including driver. Himachal entry tax, toll and parking are extra.

Is there a Himachal entry tax for taxis?

Yes, Himachal Pradesh charges an entry tax for commercial vehicles, paid at actuals at the border.

Can I do Shimla and Kufri in one day?

Yes. A day round trip leaving Chandigarh by 7 AM easily covers Shimla Mall Road and Kufri.
