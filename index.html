FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book a cab or self-drive car with BroDrive?

Fill in the booking form above to see your fare, then tap Book on WhatsApp – your trip details are sent to us automatically. You can also call us 24×7. We confirm the car, driver details and final fare within minutes.

What is included in the cab fare?

The car, fuel and driver are included. Driver allowance (₹300/day for most cars) is shown separately in the estimate. Toll, parking, state entry tax and 5% GST are extra at actuals.

What is the minimum km for one way and round trips?

One-way outstation trips are billed on a minimum of 130 km. Round trips are billed on a minimum of 250 km per calendar day. Trips under 40 km are flat city/airport transfers.

Is self-drive available outside Chandigarh?

Self-drive cars are picked up and returned in Chandigarh only, with optional doorstep delivery inside the city. Once you have the car you can drive to Punjab, Haryana or Himachal – just tell us your plan.

What documents do I need for a self-drive car?

An original driving licence at least 1 year old, Aadhaar card, and one more ID (PAN/passport/voter ID). Minimum age is 21 (23–25 for premium SUVs). A refundable security deposit is collected at pickup.

When is the security deposit refunded?

Within 3–7 working days after return, after deducting any fuel shortfall, traffic challans, late fees or damage as per our policy.

What is the daily km limit on self-drive cars?

Each 24-hour day includes 240 km (120 km for a 12-hour rental). Extra km are ₹9–₹15/km depending on the car, or choose the unlimited km add-on.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cabs: free cancellation up to 6 hours before pickup. Self drive: free up to 24 hours before pickup. See our policies page for details.
