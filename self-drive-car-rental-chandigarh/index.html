      Self-drive in Chandigarh, minus the hassle

      Chandigarh is made for driving – wide sector roads, leafy V3s and the hills just 30 minutes away. With a BroDrive self-drive car you skip the taxi wait and set your own schedule: a day trip to Kasauli, a weekend in Shimla, or just a week of running errands while your own car is in the workshop.

      How self-drive booking works

      1. Pick your car and dates above and tap Book on WhatsApp.
      2. Share photos of your driving licence and Aadhaar for quick verification.
      3. Collect the car or get it delivered to your doorstep in Chandigarh. Pay rent + refundable deposit by UPI or card.
      4. Drive! Return the car at the same fuel level; your deposit is refunded within 3–7 working days.

      Documents & eligibility

      • Original driving licence, at least 1 year old.
      • Aadhaar card + one more ID (PAN, passport or voter ID).
      • Minimum age 21 (23+ for SUVs, Thar and 7-seaters; 25+ for Innova Crysta).
      • Outstation students and tourists: additional local reference may be requested.

      Self-drive FAQs

      Is self-drive available outside Chandigarh?

      Self-drive cars are picked up and returned in Chandigarh only, with optional doorstep delivery inside the city. Once you have the car you can drive to Punjab, Haryana or Himachal – just tell us your plan.

      What documents do I need for a self-drive car?

      An original driving licence at least 1 year old, Aadhaar card, and one more ID (PAN/passport/voter ID). Minimum age is 21 (23–25 for premium SUVs). A refundable security deposit is collected at pickup.

      When is the security deposit refunded?

      Within 3–7 working days after return, after deducting any fuel shortfall, traffic challans, late fees or damage as per our policy.

      What is the daily km limit on self-drive cars?

      Each 24-hour day includes 240 km (120 km for a 12-hour rental). Extra km are ₹9–₹15/km depending on the car, or choose the unlimited km add-on.

      Can I take a self-drive car to Shimla or Manali?

      Yes. Let us know your route at booking. Himachal entry tax, tolls and any permits are paid by you. We recommend an SUV for mountain roads in winter.

      Is there a 12-hour self-drive plan?

      Yes – 12-hour rentals start at ₹1,199 with 120 free km.
