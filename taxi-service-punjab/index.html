      Punjab cab FAQs

      Do you provide cabs from anywhere in Punjab?

      Most trips start or end in the Chandigarh tricity. For pickups in other Punjab cities, enter the distance in the fare calculator or message us – we'll confirm the fare.

      Can I book a gurudwara yatra taxi?

      Yes. A round trip covering Anandpur Sahib, Kiratpur Sahib and Fatehgarh Sahib in a sedan works out to about ₹3,660 for one day.

      Do you charge Punjab state tax?

      Travel within Punjab and Chandigarh has no extra state permit. For other states, the state entry tax is extra at actuals.
